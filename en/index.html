The homelab

Two Raspberry Pi 3 B+ in a rack with a gigabit switch, running Raspberry Pi OS Lite. Each service is a systemd unit written in Python, with its own web panel and a native Android app that receives alerts.

Nothing is exposed to the internet: all access goes through a Tailscale VPN with token authentication. The APKs are built by GitHub Actions on every push, and Pi Monitor keeps an eye on everything.

● pi-monitor.service active (running)

Pi Monitor

Monitoring and control of the Raspberry Pis from my phone. A lightweight agent on each Pi exposes its state and its services, and an Android app brings it all together and alerts me when something fails.

  • CPU, temperature, RAM, SD and voltage metrics, with 24-hour and 7-day charts.
  • Detects failed or hung services and restarts them automatically, with a daily retry limit.
  • Tells “the Pi is down” apart from “the phone has no VPN” to avoid false alarms.

Market analysis with automatic insights

● wallabot.service active (running)

Wallabot

A 24/7 tracker for Wallapop, Spain's second-hand marketplace, with real-time alerts and market analysis. It follows searches, spots deals, price drops and sales, and notifies its own Android app with no third-party services.

  • Deals computed against each search's median price, with seller-urgency and risk signals.
  • Stores every listing and re-checks it to measure what sells, how fast and at what real price.
  • Real-time events over long-polling and separate Android notification channels.

● opotest.service active (running)

OpoTest TIC

A continuous-learning quiz platform for Spain's public-sector IT civil service exams (A1, A2 and C1). It combines a question bank, the indexed syllabus and AI-generated questions.

  • Spaced repetition: every missed question comes back after 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30 days.
  • New questions with Gemini, focused on the weakest topics. A second model solves them blind and discards ambiguous ones.
  • Searchable, indexed PDF syllabus, with OCR for scanned documents.

● oporadar.service active (running)

OpoRadar

A daily radar for public-sector IT job openings. Every morning it reads Spain's official state gazette (BOE) and several regional gazettes, keeps only IT positions, scores them by interest and alerts its own Android app.

  • Reads 5 gazettes: BOE (official API), BOC, BOPA, BOCYL and BOPV.
  • Classifies group, selection process and notice type, and scores from 0 to 100, explaining where each point comes from.
  • Self-monitoring: if a gazette changes its website or stops returning results, it raises a health alert.

Diecast Radar

Watches releases and preorders of 1:64 scale collectible cars on manufacturer sites and Spanish stores, and pings my phone within seconds.
